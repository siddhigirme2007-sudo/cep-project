How MediExplain AI works

Every step stays close to what your report already says. Nothing is invented, and nothing is diagnosed.

01

Optical character recognition

Tesseract/EasyOCR, with OpenCV image cleanup first, reads the text on your scanned or photographed report.

02

Data extraction

A fast text-parsing routine finds each test's name, value, unit, and the reference range printed alongside it.

03

AI explanation

Only the extracted numbers are handed to the explanation step — never asked to guess a disease or a treatment.

04

Translation & voice

The explanation is translated into your chosen language and read aloud through a Listen button.

Safety & disclaimer

{{ safety_disclaimer }}

  • Does not diagnose any disease or condition.
  • Does not name a possible disease based on a result.
  • Does not prescribe medicines, dosages, or treatment.
  • Never invents a reference range that isn't printed on your report.
  • Clearly labels a result "Unable to determine" rather than guessing.
  • Always shows the reference range exactly as printed on your report.
  • Always encourages you to consult a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Keeps your uploaded file only for the moment it's being analyzed.
